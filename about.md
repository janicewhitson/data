Behind every business question is data. Working primarily with Excel and Power BI, I focus on building reports and data models to turn that data into answers people can actually use.

My background in client operations, licensing, and sales support shapes how I approach business intelligence. I've experienced first-hand how reporting can be technically accurate yet still leave important parts of the business invisible.

My approach is grounded in a few key principles:

- Metrics only matter if everyone is working from the same definition.
- Dashboards should reflect how decisions are made.
- Clean design matters, but so do performance and long-term maintainability.
