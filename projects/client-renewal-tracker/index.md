## Getting Ahead of the Problem

As part of my Client Operations role, I managed Adobe subscription renewals for a client with approximately 500 subscriptions across multiple divisions and business units.

The client submitted separate purchase orders across its divisions and business units (and sometimes even per project), creating a risk that the combined quantities would exceed existing licence entitlements. Such discrepancies could result in rejected orders, administrative rework and potential disruption to the client's employees.

Drawing on years of experience with similarly structured accounts, I recognised this risk early and independently developed a renewal tracker.

## Beyond Order Reconciliation

Using entitlement and licence assignment data from the publisher's licensing portal, I prepared a single renewal quote and identified potential reductions and cost-saving opportunities for the client. As purchase orders arrived, I recorded them in the tracker, monitoring cumulative quantities against existing entitlements to identify discrepancies before submission.

With each annual renewal, I expanded the workbook to incorporate additional metrics, including year-over-year growth.

The labour-intensive nature of the account prompted a broader question: how commercially significant was this renewal to the overall account? I incorporated the publisher's share of the client's total purchasing activity, measured by both order volume and revenue, into the annual summaries shared with the Account Executive.

## The Value of Keeping Track

The tracker served its original purpose: no Adobe renewal orders were rejected throughout my time managing this account.

What began as a personal working document ultimately provided a clearer understanding of the client's purchasing history and the commercial significance of the annual renewal.

## Portfolio Rebuild

Reconstructed from memory using synthetic data, the workbook retains the original structure and calculation logic, including structured Excel tables and dynamic cross-worksheet calculations.
